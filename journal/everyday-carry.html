Everyday carry

The Art of Everyday Carry

A practical way to decide what earns a place in your pocket or bag.

Empty the bag first

Lay out everything you carried yesterday. Keep the things you used and anything you’d genuinely need if the day changed. Move the rest to a drawer for a week. You’ll know what to bring back by what you reach for.

Give small things a fixed place

Put keys where your hand finds them first. Keep tickets, a charging cable and loose adapters together. A small pouch makes it easier to transfer the same kit from your work bag to a weekend tote.

Pack for the day ahead

Check the destination, weather and time you’ll be out. Add what that specific day needs. A bag you can pack in a minute is usually one you’ve edited well.
