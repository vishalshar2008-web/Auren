Perspective

Five Essentials for a More Considered Everyday

A short checklist for building a practical collection of things you reach for often.

A wallet and a pair of frames

Choose a wallet for your regular cards and a frame that sits comfortably on your nose and behind your ears. Keep the sunglasses case somewhere easy to reach when you take them off.

A tote and an organiser

Check that the tote fits the largest item you routinely carry, such as a laptop. Keep loose items in a smaller case you can lift out together when you change bags.

A belt that works with your wardrobe

Try it with the trousers you wear most. Check the width of their belt loops and measure a belt you already own from the buckle to the hole you use.
