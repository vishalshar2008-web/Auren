Shipping, returns, product care and the practical details.

AUREN ships within India for this concept storefront. Standard delivery typically takes 3–7 working days after dispatch.

Returns are accepted within 7 days for unused items in original condition. Product packaging should be retained for the return journey.

Keep leather away from prolonged moisture and heat. Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth and condition sparingly when needed.

Displayed prices are presented as consumer-facing prices for this concept storefront. Final checkout tax and invoice treatment would be implemented with a live commerce stack.

Reach customer care as soon as possible after placing an order. Once dispatch has started, edits may no longer be possible.
